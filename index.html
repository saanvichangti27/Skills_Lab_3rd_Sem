SPAIN

Barcelona

Nestled along the sunny Mediterranean coast as the vibrant capital of Catalonia, Barcelona is a mesmerizing blend of ancient history and visionary art and is a must visit when exploring spain

Madrid

Madrid, the vibrant capital of Spain, is a dynamic metropolis that seamlessly blends rich historical heritage with a modern, high-energy lifestyle. Located in the center of the Iberian Peninsula, it is renowned for its world-class art museums

Seville

Seville, the vibrant capital of Spain’s Andalusia region, is a city where rich history meets lively modern culture. Famous for its sun-drenched streets and intoxicating scent of orange blossoms, the city is home to three UNESCO World Heritage sites

The Alhambra

A majestic yet calm palace complex located in Granada and a popular must visit tourist spot while visiting Spain.
